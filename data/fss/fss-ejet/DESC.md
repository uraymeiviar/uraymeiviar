# Embraer E-Jets Family (MSFS)

**Role:** Core Systems & Avionics Engineer  
**Domain / Tags:** FMS, Autopilot, Flight Dynamics, Avionics, C++, Cockpit Systems, MSFS SDK

## Executive Summary
Comprehensive systems, FMS navigation, autopilot flight director laws, and cockpit display engineering for the E170, E175, E190, E195, and E-Freighter.

## Key Technical Highlights
- Flight Management System (FMS): Waypoint sequencing, lateral/vertical navigation, NavData ARINC-424 cycle parsing, and takeoff/climb performance calculation (V1, VR, V2, thrust limits).
- Autopilot & Autoflight Management: Pitch/Roll guidance (LNAV, VNAV PTH/FLCH), ILS/Autoland CAT II/III approach tracking, and autothrottle system.
- Cockpit Display Vector Rendering: Primary Flight Display (PFD) speed/altitude tapes, Multi-Function Display (MFD) moving map, and EICAS engine and alert messaging.
- Deep Systems Simulation: Multi-bus AC/DC electrical distribution with bus tie logic, 3-circuit hydraulic system with engine/electric pumps and PTU, and pneumatic/pressurization loops.
